A mini game dev team

We're two ex-AAA game developers going indie. Classic story. We love making games, and want to get back to our roots of making delightful little experiences for people to enjoy. We love the handcrafted art of making video games, and we think it shows in the stuff we're putting together. It's all very human, even if it means there's some rough edges!

What is our first game as indies?

If you like cozy little stories, and tippy little trains, you're going to love what we're working on. It's unofficially deemed "Train Game" internally, but we're working on publishing the real steam page with the real "Train Game Name" soon(tm). We can't wait to share what we're working on!

How can you stay up to date?

If you don't want to miss out, or you're curious and want to come chat, join our discord. That's our best avenue to connect with you! Come say hi, and wish us luck on our new journey as indie devs. We're gonna need it! 😅
